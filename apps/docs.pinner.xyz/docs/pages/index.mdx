## What is Pinner?

**Pinner is:** a decentralized storage service that pins your content and respects your privacy. Your data lives on a distributed network; no single point of failure, no vendor lock-in. One subscription covers pinning, website hosting, and DNS.

**Pinner is not:** a public anonymous IPFS gateway or a lock-in platform. We don't serve arbitrary IPFS content to the public; see [Why No Public Gateway](/ipfs/concepts/why-no-gateway) for the reasoning. Website hosting serves your pinned content through ipfs.pub.
