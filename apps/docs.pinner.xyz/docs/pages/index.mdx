## At a Glance

**What we provide:**
- Content pinning across multiple decentralized storage networks
- Privacy-focused infrastructure
- Competitive, transparent pricing

**What we don't provide:**
- Public HTTP gateways for content retrieval
- CDN or low-latency content delivery

## Why Pinner?

[Why choose Pinner?](/about)

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **No Gateway Subsidies** | You pay for pinning, not cross-subsidized gateway infrastructure |
| **Clear Content Rules** | We happily pin any data that follows our terms of service. Your content is yours, we stay aligned to you as our customer |
| **Simple Pricing** | Straightforward tiers with no hidden fees |
| **Multi-Network** | Support for multiple storage networks through one service |
